# Week 4 – Exploratory Data Analysis and Feature Engineering

## Project Overview

This project focuses on Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) and Feature Engineering using a Sephora skincare customer reviews dataset. The main objective is to understand customer feedback, identify patterns in product ratings and prices, analyze brand performance, and explore customer review trends.

## Objectives

- Understand and explore the customer review dataset.
- Perform statistical analysis and data visualization.
- Analyze customer ratings and product price distributions.
- Explore brand-wise reviews and customer skin types.
- Identify trends in customer reviews over time.
- Apply feature engineering techniques to create useful new variables.

## Tools and Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Visual Studio Code
- Microsoft Excel

## Exploratory Data Analysis

The project includes the following analysis:

1. Distribution of customer ratings.
2. Top 10 most-reviewed brands.
3. Distribution of product prices.
4. Relationship between product prices and ratings.
5. Average rating by recommendation status.
6. Review helpfulness by rating.
7. Distribution of customer skin types.
8. Customer review trends over time.
9. Average ratings of the top 10 most-reviewed brands.

## Feature Engineering

The following features and techniques were implemented:

- Review length calculation.
- Extraction of review year and month.
- Product price categorization.
- Customer rating categorization.
- Min-Max normalization.
- One-hot encoding.
- Price-rating interaction feature.

## Key Findings

- Five-star reviews were the most common.
- Mid-range products received the highest number of reviews.
- March recorded the highest number of reviews.
- Combination skin was the most frequently recorded skin type.
- Paula's Choice had the highest average rating among the ten most-reviewed brands.

## Final Dataset

After feature engineering, the dataset contained **49,972 rows and 35 columns**.
